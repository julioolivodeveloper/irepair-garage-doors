Top Garage Door Brands in 2026: Best Options for San Antonio, Texas

📅 April 22, 2026 · ✍️ iRepair Garage Doors · 📍 San Antonio, TX
← Back to Blog

Walk into any big-box store and you'll find garage doors from brands you've never heard of at prices that seem too good to be true. Sometimes they are. As technicians who install and repair doors from every brand on the market, we have real opinions — based on thousands of installations — about what holds up in Texas and what doesn't.

Here's our honest assessment of the top four garage door brands for San Antonio homeowners in 2026.

What to Look for in a Texas Climate

Before we dive into brands, here's what matters most for a San Antonio home:

  • Heat resistance: Can the material handle 100°F+ temperatures without warping or degrading?
  • Insulation (R-value): Texas summers demand high insulation. R-13 minimum; R-16 to R-18 preferred.
  • Durability and dent resistance: SA gets hail. Steel gauge matters.
  • Warranty: A quality brand stands behind its product. Lifetime warranties on steel components are the gold standard.
  • Parts availability: If a panel needs to be replaced in 10 years, can you still get matching panels?

Top 4 Garage Door Brands for San Antonio in 2026

1. Clopay — Best Overall

Clopay is the largest door manufacturer in the US and, in our opinion, the most consistently excellent. Their Gallery and Coachman collections offer stunning aesthetics; their Sage and Canyon Ridge lines are workhorses for everyday homes. Most Clopay doors are manufactured in the US (Troy, Ohio), and their weather-resistance is excellent for Texas conditions. Their ENERGY STAR-certified insulated doors are among the best-performing in hot climates. We install more Clopay than any other brand.

Best for: Homeowners who want the widest selection and reliable quality at a mid-to-premium price.

2. Amarr — Best Insulation for Texas

Amarr specializes in steel doors and their insulation options are among the best in the industry. Their Classica and Hillcrest lines are popular in San Antonio for good reason — they're built for demanding climates and offer up to R-18 insulation. Amarr is a Winston-Salem, NC company with strong distribution in Texas. Their Heritage collection is one of the most popular designs we see on SA homes right now.

Best for: Homeowners prioritizing energy efficiency and insulation performance.

3. C.H.I. Overhead Doors — Best for American-Made Quality

C.H.I. (Celina, Ohio) is a favorite among professional installers for their build quality and finish consistency. Their steel is gauge-tested, their paint finishes resist fading well in direct Texas sun, and their warranty is comprehensive. The 2216 and 2283 lines are workhorses we've installed dozens of times in SA. If you value American manufacturing and solid warranties, CHI is a strong choice.

Best for: Homeowners who want US-made quality and want their door to look great for 20+ years.

4. Wayne Dalton — Best Budget-Friendly Option

Wayne Dalton (now part of Overhead Door Corporation) offers solid entry-level to mid-range doors at competitive prices. Their Model 9100 and 9605 are good starter doors for replacement situations where budget is the primary constraint. Build quality is decent for the price, though insulation options are more limited compared to Clopay or Amarr at the premium end. Still a better choice than unbranded import doors.

Best for: Homeowners on a budget who still want a recognizable brand with parts availability.
