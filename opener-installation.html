Opener Types

Which Opener Is Right for You?

We install all major types of garage door openers. Edgar will help you choose the best option for your door, budget, and lifestyle.

Most Popular

Belt Drive

The quietest option — ideal for homes with living space above or next to the garage. Smooth, reliable, and low-maintenance.

  • Ultra-quiet operation
  • Best for attached garages
  • Long lifespan, minimal maintenance
  • Available with battery backup

Chain Drive

The most affordable and durable option. Perfect for detached garages or heavier doors where noise isn't a concern.

  • Most affordable option
  • Handles heavy doors
  • Extremely durable
  • Easy to service and maintain

Smart Wi-Fi Opener

Control your garage from anywhere using the myQ app. Receive alerts when your door opens and close it remotely.

  • Control from your smartphone
  • Real-time alerts and history
  • Works with Alexa & Google Home
  • Built-in camera options available

FAQ

Opener Installation Questions

How long does opener installation take? +

Most garage door opener installations take 1–2 hours. Edgar has done many in under 30 minutes. We include all programming time in that estimate.

Which opener brand do you recommend? +

We primarily install LiftMaster and Chamberlain — both are exceptionally reliable, have excellent warranties, and are backed by the best smart home ecosystem (myQ). We'll recommend the specific model that fits your needs and budget.

Can you program my car's built-in garage button? +

Yes — HomeLink programming is included with every installation. Just tell us your vehicle make and model and we'll set it up before we leave.

My opener is old — can you just repair it instead? +

Often yes. If your opener is under 15 years old and the motor still works, we may be able to repair it for less than a replacement. Call Edgar and he'll give you an honest assessment of repair vs. replace.
