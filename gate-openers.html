FAQ

Gate Opener Questions

Can you install a gate opener on my existing gate? +

In most cases yes — we assess your existing gate during the estimate to confirm compatibility. Most swing and sliding gates can be fitted with an automatic opener without major modifications.

How long does installation take? +

A standard residential gate opener installation takes 2–3 hours. More complex systems with intercoms or multiple gates may take longer.

Do you program the car's built-in HomeLink system? +

Yes — Edgar can program your vehicle's built-in HomeLink or visor clip to control your gate opener as part of the installation.

What happens if the power goes out? +

We can install battery backup systems that keep your gate operational during power outages. Manual release mechanisms are also included on all systems.
