FAQ

Spring Repair Questions

How do I know if my spring is broken? +

Signs include: a loud bang from the garage (the spring snapping), door that won't open or opens only partially, visible gap in the coil above the door, or a door that feels extremely heavy when lifted manually.

Should I replace one spring or both? +

We recommend replacing both springs at the same time. Springs wear at the same rate, so if one breaks, the other will likely fail soon. Replacing both saves you a second service call and ensures balanced tension on your door.

How long do garage door springs last? +

Standard springs are rated for about 10,000 cycles (one open + one close = one cycle). For most families that's 7–10 years. High-cycle springs rated for 25,000+ cycles are available if you want longer life.

Is it safe to drive my car out with a broken spring? +

No — operating a garage door with a broken spring puts excessive strain on the opener motor and cables, and can cause additional damage. Keep the door closed and call us for same-day service.
