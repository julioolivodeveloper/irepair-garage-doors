7 Signs It's Time to Replace Your Garage Door

📅 July 20, 2024 · ✍️ iRepair Garage Doors · 📍 San Antonio, TX
← Back to Blog

A well-maintained garage door can last 25-30 years. But there comes a point where continuing to repair it is just throwing money away. Here are the 7 signs that it's time to replace it.

1. Visible structural damage

Bent, cracked, or deeply dented panels affect insulation and security. If only one or two panels are damaged, they can be replaced individually. If several are affected, a new door is the more cost-effective option.

2. The door is excessively noisy

A mild squeak that goes away with lubrication is normal. But if you hear loud rattling, banging, or constant grinding even after maintenance, the tracks, springs, and hinges are at the end of their service life.

3. It moves slowly or unevenly

A door that goes down on one side before the other has unbalanced springs or cables. This causes accelerated wear on every component.

4. More than 2 repairs in one year

If you've already paid for two or more repairs in the last 12 months, the cumulative costs exceed the price of a new door. Make one smart investment instead.

5. Poor insulation

In San Antonio's climate, a hot garage can raise the temperature inside your home. Old doors without proper thermal insulation can significantly increase your electricity bill.

6. The appearance is an eyesore

The garage door accounts for about 30% of your home's curb appeal. An old, worn-out door lowers your property's resale value. A new door can recover up to 98% of its cost in added home value.

7. It's over 20 years old

Doors from that era lack modern safety features: auto-reverse sensors, anti-intrusion locking, and heavier-gauge panels. A modern door protects your home far better.
