FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a new door installation take? +

Most residential garage door installations take 2–4 hours from start to finish. We handle removal of the old door, installation of the new one, and a full test of the opener system before we leave.

Do you offer same-day service? +

Yes — same-day appointments are available on most days. Call or text Edgar in the morning and we'll do our best to schedule you that same day.

What styles and materials do you carry? +

We carry steel (raised panel, flush, carriage-house style), wood-look composite, and full-view aluminum/glass doors. Both insulated and non-insulated options are available in various colors and window configurations.

Will you take away my old door? +

Absolutely. We remove and haul away your old door as part of the installation service — no extra charge.

How much does a new garage door cost? +

Pricing depends on size, material, and style. We offer free estimates — call Edgar and he'll give you a clear number before any work starts. No pressure, no hidden fees.
